5. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Project files are stored locally on your device in the App's private application directory. Other applications cannot read these files.
  • If you sign in, copies of your projects, account profile, and autosave records are stored in Cloud Firestore on Google's servers.
  • International transfers: data may be processed in the United States. Where this occurs, Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to safeguard your data.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data & cloud projects: retained until you delete your account.
  • Crash logs: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics data: retained by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months (this is the default retention setting we have configured).
  • Local device data: retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data from inside the App, in Account → Delete account. This action is irreversible.
  • Right to restrict processing.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. EU users may consult the list of national authorities.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mihaitrader56@gmail.com with the subject Flipa Studio — Privacy Request. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • All network traffic between the App and our service providers is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — Firebase Authentication stores them as salted hashes.
  • Cloud project data is protected by Firestore security rules so that each user can only read and write their own data.
  • Despite reasonable safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for general audiences but is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

For more information on Google's ads practices, see Google's Ad Privacy & Terms.
